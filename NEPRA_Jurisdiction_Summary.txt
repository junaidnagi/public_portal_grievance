NEPRA JURISDICTION SUMMARY
For RAG / CrewAI complaint classification
Last reviewed: 03 October 2026

AUTHORITY
National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA)

PRIMARY ROLE
NEPRA is the electricity-sector regulator. Its Complaint Management System and Complaint Handling & Dispute Resolution Procedure Rules, 2015 cover consumer complaints and disputes involving electricity licensees. The Consumer Service Manual applies to consumers served by Distribution Companies (DISCOs) and Bulk Power Consumers.

IMPORTANT ROUTING PRINCIPLE
For an ordinary electricity-service problem, the consumer should normally first approach the relevant Distribution Company (for example IESCO) and the concerned SDO/XEN. NEPRA's current complaint-registration page specifically advises consumers to complain to the concerned SDO/XEN before lodging the matter with NEPRA.

ROUTE TO NEPRA WHEN
- The matter is an electricity consumer dispute involving a licensed electricity utility/DISCO
- The consumer has already approached the relevant DISCO/SDO/XEN and the issue remains unresolved
- The complaint concerns regulatory compliance, consumer rights or failure by the DISCO to resolve the electricity complaint
- Billing, meter, connection or supply disputes have reached the regulatory-escalation stage

COMMON KEYWORDS
NEPRA, DISCO, electricity regulator, unresolved electricity complaint, billing dispute,
detection bill, meter dispute, electricity connection dispute, SDO, XEN, IESCO complaint unresolved.

DO NOT ROUTE DIRECTLY TO NEPRA FOR A ROUTINE FIRST-TIME IESCO SERVICE COMPLAINT
Examples:
- First report of local power failure
- First complaint about low voltage
- First complaint about defective meter
- First request to correct a bill
- New connection/service request

These should normally go first to IESCO/relevant DISCO.

ONE-YEAR PORTAL RULE
NEPRA's current complaint-registration page states that a complaint beyond one year cannot be processed under the NEPRA Complaint Handling and Dispute Resolution Procedure Rules, 2015 through that mechanism.

RECOMMENDED APP LOGIC
1. Detect that complaint concerns electricity.
2. Determine the relevant DISCO from city/area, if possible.
3. Ask/check whether consumer already complained to the DISCO/SDO/XEN.
4. If NO -> route to IESCO/relevant DISCO first.
5. If YES and unresolved/disputed -> route/escalate to NEPRA.
6. Record how old the issue is.
7. If over one year, flag that NEPRA's current portal states it cannot be processed under the 2015 rules through that mechanism.

MINIMUM INFORMATION TO COLLECT
- Complainant name
- City/address/area
- Electricity DISCO
- Consumer/reference number
- Complaint description
- Billing month / meter details where relevant
- Date issue started
- Whether complaint was already made to DISCO
- Complaint/reference number from DISCO, if available
- Result/response from DISCO
- Supporting bills, notices, meter photos or correspondence

CLASSIFICATION EXAMPLES
"My IESCO bill is wrong and I have not complained yet." -> IESCO first, not NEPRA first
"I complained to IESCO and the XEN but my billing dispute remains unresolved." -> NEPRA escalation
"My meter stopped working yesterday." -> IESCO first
"IESCO rejected my repeated complaint about an incorrect detection bill." -> NEPRA escalation

PEMRA JURISDICTION SUMMARY
For RAG / CrewAI complaint classification
Last reviewed: 03 October 2026

AUTHORITY
Pakistan Electronic Media Regulatory Authority (PEMRA)

PRIMARY ROLE
PEMRA regulates licensed electronic media. Its Council of Complaints is a forum for complaints concerning programmes broadcast or distributed by PEMRA licensees. Under the 2023 amendment, Councils of Complaints also handle certain grievances of electronic-media employees concerning non-payment of wages, salaries and dues.

ROUTE TO PEMRA WHEN THE COMPLAINT INVOLVES
- Television programme content broadcast by a PEMRA licensee
- Radio / FM broadcast content
- Cable television service/content involving a PEMRA licensee
- Programme standards and alleged Code of Conduct violations
- Broadcast advertisements
- Other matters involving licensed electronic-media broadcasters/distributors
- Certain electronic-media employee complaints regarding unpaid wages, salaries or dues

COMMON KEYWORDS
PEMRA, TV channel, television, programme, broadcast, FM radio, radio station,
cable operator, cable TV, advertisement, ad, anchor, talk show, drama, news channel,
broadcast content, electronic media, wages of media employee.

DO NOT ROUTE TO PEMRA AS THE PRIMARY AUTHORITY WHEN
- Mobile telecom, SIM, signal, broadband telecom, DIRBS or IMEI issue -> PTA.
- Electricity supply/billing/meter/new connection -> IESCO or relevant DISCO; NEPRA for regulatory escalation.
- Complaint concerns ordinary internet connectivity rather than broadcast media -> usually PTA.

COUNCIL OF COMPLAINTS WORKFLOW
1. Complaint is lodged against a relevant aspect of a programme or service of a PEMRA licensee.
2. Council of Complaints receives/reviews the complaint.
3. Relevant parties/licensee may be heard or called for explanation according to applicable procedure.
4. Council forms an opinion/recommendation.
5. Recommendation goes to PEMRA for appropriate decision/order.

MINIMUM INFORMATION TO COLLECT IN THE APP
- Complainant name
- City
- Complaint description
- TV/radio/cable channel or licensee name
- Programme/advertisement name, if known
- Date and approximate time of broadcast
- Specific content complained of
- Screenshot, video clip, transcript, link or other evidence, if available
- Whether complaint concerns employee wages/salary/dues

CLASSIFICATION EXAMPLES
"A TV channel broadcast objectionable material in a talk show." -> PEMRA / Programme content
"An FM station aired an inappropriate advertisement." -> PEMRA / Broadcast advertisement
"My cable operator is a licensed PEMRA service and I have a service/content complaint." -> PEMRA
"My mobile signal is weak." -> NOT PEMRA; PTA
"My electricity meter is defective." -> NOT PEMRA; IESCO/relevant DISCO

PTA JURISDICTION SUMMARY
For RAG / CrewAI complaint classification
Last reviewed: 03 October 2026

AUTHORITY
Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA)

PRIMARY ROLE
PTA regulates telecommunication services in Pakistan. Its Complaint Management System (CMS) allows telecom consumers to lodge and track complaints relating to telecommunications services.

ROUTE TO PTA WHEN THE COMPLAINT INVOLVES
- Mobile/cellular service quality
- Weak or unavailable mobile signal / coverage
- Mobile internet/data service
- Telecom billing, packages, balance deductions or service charges
- SIM-related telecom service problems
- Fixed-line telecom service
- Internet service provider / broadband telecom complaints
- DIRBS / mobile device registration issues
- IMEI-related issues
- Stolen handset blocking or recovered handset unblocking
- Fraudulent mobile-number activity
- Spam, unsolicited or fraudulent telecom communications
- Certain unlawful online-content complaints handled through PTA mechanisms

COMMON KEYWORDS
PTA, mobile network, SIM, signal, coverage, call drop, mobile data, internet provider,
broadband, telecom bill, package, balance, IMEI, DIRBS, PTA approved phone,
stolen mobile, block IMEI, unblock IMEI, scam call, fraudulent number, spam SMS.

DO NOT ROUTE TO PTA AS THE PRIMARY AUTHORITY WHEN
- Complaint is about electricity supply, billing, meter or connection -> IESCO / relevant DISCO, with NEPRA as regulator/escalation authority.
- Complaint is about television/radio broadcast programming, licensed electronic-media content, or cable-TV content -> PEMRA.
- Complaint is simply about a general consumer product with no telecom-service issue.

PTA CMS WORKFLOW
1. Consumer creates/uses PTA CMS profile.
2. Complaint is submitted with complaint details.
3. Complaint is routed/processed through the relevant complaint mechanism.
4. Consumer can monitor status.
5. Updates are provided through CMS.
6. Consumer can provide feedback.

MINIMUM INFORMATION TO COLLECT IN THE APP
- Complainant name
- City
- Complaint description
- Telecom operator/service provider, if known
- Mobile/telephone/account number where relevant
- Complaint category
- Date/time of issue
- Evidence or screenshots, if relevant
- IMEI for handset-related complaints
- URL/platform/screenshot for unlawful-online-content complaints, if relevant

CLASSIFICATION EXAMPLES
"My Jazz SIM has no signal in my area." -> PTA / Mobile network coverage
"My broadband provider keeps overbilling me." -> PTA / Telecom billing-service complaint
"My imported phone IMEI is blocked by DIRBS." -> PTA / DIRBS-IMEI
"My phone was stolen and I want the IMEI blocked." -> PTA / Stolen handset-IMEI
"I received a scam SMS from a mobile number." -> PTA / Fraudulent communication
"My electricity bill is wrong." -> NOT PTA; electricity authority
"A TV channel broadcast objectionable content." -> NOT PTA; PEMRA
